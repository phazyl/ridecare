Geometric ceiling at this mix–
Geometric ceiling = packing limit × road area ÷ mean vehicle footprint. It is a space limit, not a traffic-flow capacity. Every vehicle is placed with an overlap check and only moves into free space, so vehicles never overlap; whatever does not fit is reported under “Placement”. The simulation runs in a background worker. If the device cannot keep up, the “Simulation rate” falls below the selected speed instead of the page freezing. This is a simplified model, not a calibrated traffic forecast.

Vehicle dimensions

Edit footprints & free-flow speeds
Defaults: motorcycle 2.0×0.8 m (UK/Singapore bay), car 4.4×1.8 m; HDC parking bays are 2.3×5 m (car) and 2×1 m (motorcycle).
Model: vehicles are placed area-weighted on the street network (wide, long streets get proportionally more) and drive on the left. Parked vehicles sit in the kerbside strip. Vehicles keep clear of junction boxes: they stop at a stop line and cross when there is room beyond. If the slowdown option is on, they slow as local coverage approaches the packing limit; with it off, they keep their free-flow speed until blocked.
