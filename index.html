Defaults: motorcycle 2.0×0.8 m (UK/Singapore bay), car 4.4×1.8 m; HDC parking bays are 2.3×5 m (car) and 2×1 m (motorcycle).
Model: vehicles are placed area-weighted on the street network (wide, long streets get proportionally more), drive on the left, and — if the slowdown option is on — slow linearly as local coverage approaches the packing limit. With it off, vehicles keep an even, area-proportional spread.
